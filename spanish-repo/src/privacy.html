Privacy Policy — Habla Libre

Last updated: October 2026

What we store and why

  • On your device: your course progress, XP, streaks and settings are saved locally in your browser. Clearing your browser data removes them.
  • Leaderboard (Firebase): if you sign in for the global leaderboard, we store the name you enter, your XP and your lesson counts on Google Firebase. This is visible to other players. Use a nickname if you prefer.
  • Analytics: we use Vercel Web Analytics, which is cookie-less and does not track you across websites.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or share your data with advertisers.
  • We don't store audio; speech practice is generated on your own device.
  • We don't require an account to use the core course.

Data requests

You can ask us to delete your leaderboard entry any time: ashok@kitchenrush.app.
